SendAble Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

SendAble is an Android application developed and operated by Pălimariu Răzvan-Ciprian, an individual developer. For privacy or support questions, contact razvanciprianp@gmail.com.

1. What SendAble does

SendAble prepares videos and images selected by the user so they can be shared more easily through third-party applications and services such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, email applications, or other Android apps.

2. Media processing

Media selected by the user is processed locally on the Android device. SendAble does not intentionally upload selected videos or images to a SendAble-owned media-processing server.

Depending on the selected operation, SendAble may split, remux, compress, convert, or otherwise prepare media files on the device.

Prepared files may be stored in the device's Download/SendAble folder until the user deletes them.

3. Information stored locally

SendAble may store information locally on the user's device, including:

  • application preferences and settings;
  • processing history and related file metadata;
  • processing recovery information;
  • share queue progress;
  • cached platform rules or configuration;
  • temporary files or cached media information required for processing.

This information is primarily stored locally and is not intentionally sent to a SendAble-owned server.

4. Internet access

SendAble uses internet access to retrieve updated platform configuration and operational sharing rules.

The current version may make HTTPS requests to infrastructure hosted by GitHub in order to retrieve SendAble platform-rule configuration.

As with ordinary internet connections, the hosting provider may automatically receive technical network information such as the user's IP address and request metadata required to provide the service. SendAble does not intentionally use this information to identify or profile individual users.

GitHub's own privacy terms apply to information processed by GitHub.

5. Sharing media with other applications

When the user chooses to share media with another application, SendAble asks Android to transfer the selected or prepared media to the destination chosen by the user.

This may include applications such as Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, email applications, or other compatible Android applications.

Once information is transferred to another application or service at the user's request, that third party's privacy policy and terms apply. SendAble does not control how those external applications process the information after the handoff.

6. Permissions

Depending on the Android version and features used, SendAble may request or use permissions and Android capabilities required for:

  • accessing media specifically selected by the user;
  • processing media in the foreground or background;
  • displaying processing or completion notifications;
  • accessing the internet to retrieve platform configuration.

SendAble does not request access to media for unrelated purposes.

7. Advertising, analytics and accounts

The current version of SendAble does not provide user accounts and does not intentionally use advertising or behavioral analytics services.

SendAble does not sell users' personal information.

If future versions introduce accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud processing, crash-reporting services that transmit user data, or other material changes in data handling, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated accordingly.

8. Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by SendAble remains on the device until it is removed by the user, removed through available application controls, deleted by Android, or removed when the application is uninstalled.

Prepared media files stored outside the application's private storage, such as files in Download/SendAble, may remain on the device after uninstalling the application and can be deleted using SendAble's available controls or the device's file-management tools.

Because SendAble does not currently provide a SendAble user account or intentionally maintain a remote user database, there is generally no remote SendAble account data requiring an account-deletion request.

9. Security

SendAble is designed to minimize transmission of user media by performing its core media-preparation operations locally on the device.

Network connections used for platform configuration use HTTPS where supported by the configured service.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

SendAble is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The application does not intentionally request a child's name, email address, account profile, precise location, or similar personal information as part of its core functionality.

11. Third-party services

SendAble may interact with third-party applications or infrastructure, including services selected by the user for sharing and hosting services used to retrieve platform configuration.

These services operate independently from SendAble and are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SendAble's features, data practices, third-party integrations, or legal requirements change.

The effective date at the top of this document will be updated when appropriate.
