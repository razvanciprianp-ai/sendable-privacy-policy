SendAble Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

SendAble is an Android application developed and operated by Pălimariu Răzvan-Ciprian, an individual developer. For privacy or support questions, contact razvanciprianp@gmail.com.

1. What SendAble does

SendAble prepares videos and images selected by the user so they can be shared more easily through third-party applications and services such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, email applications, or other Android apps.

2. Media processing

Media selected by the user is processed locally on the Android device. SendAble does not intentionally upload selected videos or images to a SendAble-owned media-processing server.

Depending on the selected operation, SendAble may split, remux, compress, convert, or otherwise prepare media files on the device.

Prepared files may be stored in the device's Download/SendAble folder until the user deletes them.

3. Information stored locally

SendAble may store information locally on the user's device, including:

  • application preferences and settings;
  • processing history and related file metadata;
  • processing recovery information;
  • share queue progress;
  • cached platform rules or configuration;
  • temporary files or cached media information required for processing.

This application-specific information is primarily stored locally and is not intentionally sent to a SendAble-owned server. Separate third-party SDKs used for advertising, consent, billing, or infrastructure may process technical information as described in the sections below.

4. Internet access

SendAble uses internet access to retrieve updated platform configuration and operational sharing rules.

The current version may make HTTPS requests to infrastructure hosted by GitHub in order to retrieve SendAble platform-rule configuration.

As with ordinary internet connections, the hosting provider may automatically receive technical network information such as the user's IP address and request metadata required to provide the service. SendAble does not intentionally use this information to identify or profile individual users.

GitHub's own privacy terms apply to information processed by GitHub.

5. Sharing media with other applications

When the user chooses to share media with another application, SendAble asks Android to transfer the selected or prepared media to the destination chosen by the user.

This may include applications such as Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, email applications, or other compatible Android applications.

Once information is transferred to another application or service at the user's request, that third party's privacy policy and terms apply. SendAble does not control how those external applications process the information after the handoff.

6. Permissions

Depending on the Android version and features used, SendAble may request or use permissions and Android capabilities required for:

  • accessing media specifically selected by the user;
  • processing media in the foreground or background;
  • displaying processing or completion notifications;
  • accessing the internet to retrieve platform configuration;
  • requesting advertising and privacy-consent information from Google services;
  • communicating with Google Play for Premium subscription information.

SendAble does not request access to media for unrelated purposes.

7. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The free version of SendAble may display advertising provided through Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Depending on the user's location, consent choices, device settings, and Google's applicable policies, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise limited.

According to Google's documentation for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. This may include:

  • the device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • user product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad/video interaction information;
  • diagnostic and performance information related to the application and advertising SDK;
  • device and account identifiers, which may include the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and other applicable identifiers.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. The Android advertising ID can also be reset or deleted through supported Android privacy controls.

SendAble does not currently integrate a separate behavioral analytics service for the developer's own analytics. Advertising-related analytics and measurement may, however, be performed by Google and its advertising partners as part of the advertising service.

SendAble Premium is intended to remove SendAble's advertising placements while the Premium entitlement is active.

8. Consent and privacy choices

SendAble integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request and manage advertising/privacy consent where Google or applicable law requires it.

For users in regions where a privacy message is required, UMP may display a consent form that allows the user to consent, refuse consent, or manage privacy options. SendAble requests ads only when the UMP consent state indicates that ad requests are permitted.

Where required by UMP, SendAble may also display a Privacy choices or Opțiuni de confidențialitate entry in Settings so the user can reopen Google's privacy-options form.

9. Subscriptions and Google Play Billing

SendAble may offer an optional Premium subscription through Google Play. Google Play handles the purchase and payment process under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

SendAble may receive subscription-related information from Google Play that is necessary to display localized pricing, determine whether a Premium entitlement is active, restore an existing subscription, and process the subscription experience in the application.

SendAble does not receive or store the user's full payment-card details. Payment-method information is handled by Google Play.

10. Accounts and sale of personal information

SendAble does not currently provide a SendAble user account system.

SendAble does not sell users' personal information.

Data processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob, Google Play, GitHub, or destination applications is subject to those services' own privacy policies and terms.

11. Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by SendAble remains on the device until it is removed by the user, removed through available application controls, deleted by Android, or removed when the application is uninstalled.

Prepared media files stored outside the application's private storage, such as files in Download/SendAble, may remain on the device after uninstalling the application and can be deleted using SendAble's available controls or the device's file-management tools.

Because SendAble does not currently provide a SendAble user account or intentionally maintain a remote user database, there is generally no remote SendAble account data requiring an account-deletion request.

12. Security

SendAble is designed to minimize transmission of user media by performing its core media-preparation operations locally on the device.

Network connections used for platform configuration and supported third-party SDK communication use secure transport where provided by the relevant service. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Children's privacy

SendAble is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The application does not intentionally request a child's name, email address, account profile, precise location, or similar personal information as part of its core functionality.

14. Third-party services

SendAble may interact with third-party applications and infrastructure, including:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy and consent management;
  • Google Play / Google Play Billing for Premium subscription purchases and entitlement information;
  • GitHub-hosted infrastructure used to retrieve SendAble platform-rule configuration;
  • applications chosen by the user for sharing, such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, email applications, or other compatible services.

These services operate independently from SendAble and are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Google's privacy information is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy and information about Google's advertising technologies is available at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SendAble's features, data practices, third-party integrations, or legal requirements change.

The effective date at the top of this document will be updated when appropriate.
